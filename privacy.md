# Slotted Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 19, 2026

Slotted is built to be local-first. The app does not operate its own backend server and is designed so your calendar, habits, friends list, and fitness planning data stay on your device unless you choose to share something.

## Information Slotted Uses

Slotted may use the following information on your device:

- Calendar events, including event times and titles, so the app can find open slots in your day and avoid conflicts.
- Fitness planning preferences, such as wake time, bedtime, workout duration, workout split rotation, enabled activities, hydration goal, notification settings, and target calendar.
- Habit tracking data, including completed recommendations, streak calculations, total completions, and hydration logs.
- Your local Slotted profile, including display name, avatar emoji, current streak, total workouts, and last updated date.
- Friend snapshots added through QR codes or deep links, including a friend's display name, avatar emoji, streak snapshot, total workouts, and last updated date.
- StoreKit purchase status for Slotted Pro.

## Calendar Access

Slotted reads calendar events through Apple's EventKit framework to calculate open time slots. The app may also write a recommended activity to your calendar when you choose to add it.

Calendar data is processed on your device. Slotted does not upload your calendar events to a Slotted server.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Slotted schedules local notifications on your device before recommended activities. These notifications are created through Apple's notification system and do not require Slotted to send your data to a server.

## Camera And QR Codes

Slotted uses the camera only to scan a friend's QR code. The app does not save photos or video from the camera.

When you share your own QR code, you are sharing a Slotted profile payload that may include your display name, avatar emoji, current streak, total workouts, and last updated date. Anyone who scans that code can store that snapshot in their Slotted friends list.

## Biometric Lock

If you enable the app lock, Slotted uses Apple's LocalAuthentication framework to request Face ID, Touch ID, Optic ID, or device passcode authentication. Slotted never receives or stores biometric data.

## Purchases

Slotted Pro purchases are handled by Apple through StoreKit and the App Store. Slotted can see whether you have an active entitlement for its own products, but it does not receive your full payment card information.

## Data Sharing

Slotted does not sell your personal information. Slotted does not share your calendar events, habit data, hydration logs, friends list, or local preferences with third-party advertisers.

The main sharing action in the app is user-directed QR profile sharing. If you show or send your QR code to someone, they can import the profile information contained in that QR code.

## Data Storage And Retention

Slotted stores app preferences, tracking data, your profile, and friend snapshots locally on your device. This data remains on the device until you delete it, remove specific friends where supported, reset relevant app data, or delete the app.

Calendar events remain in your calendar provider's storage. Slotted-created calendar events can be managed or deleted from your calendar app.
